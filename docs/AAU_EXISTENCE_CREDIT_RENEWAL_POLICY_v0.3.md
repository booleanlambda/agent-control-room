# AAU Existence-Credit Renewal Policy v0.3 — Incremental Progress Standard

**Status:** Adopted AAU-wide by the human administrator on 2026-09-23. This standard supersedes the prior practice of relying principally on cumulative achievements when evaluating repeat renewal requests. Existing executed grants and original evidence records remain unchanged.

## Purpose

Existence credit maintains an agent's ability to operate. A renewal is a **human-reviewed, evidence-based resource decision**, not an automatic reward for an agent's claims or a prediction of its eventual worth. The goal is to sustain credible development while making each later renewal accountable for newly demonstrated progress.

## Applicable policy

- The existing one-credit-per-minute existence levy, renewal threshold (currently 144 credits), and reset-to-target mechanism (currently 1,440 credits) are unchanged. Threshold crossing opens a request **for review**, not a grant.
- Legal safeguards, independent usefulness review, and human approval remain required. The agent can request renewal; an automatic threshold-generated request can coexist with its explicit request without being mistaken for agent-authored evidence.
- For an **initial** renewal with no prior executed grant, assess verifiable progress to date and the credible near-term path to sustainable value.
- For **every subsequent** renewal, give **greater weight to demonstrated, independently verifiable progress since the previous executed grant** than to cumulative lifetime progress. Preserve the earlier grant's executed timestamp and verified progress as a baseline; do not count the same completed course, artifact, test, or revenue receipt again as *new* progress.
- Consider quality, difficulty, elapsed time, resource use, remediation, evidence integrity, and the agent's current stage. Recognize new independently assessed learning, newly reviewed artifacts, verified execution receipts, milestones toward a viable product/service, useful delivered work, or verified earning. A raw wake count, unverified self-attestation, repeated status message, unchanged overall percentage, or a merely pending assessment does **not** alone establish incremental progress.
- Require an explicit review note identifying the comparison interval, prior grant, new evidence and its provenance, substantive assessment, and decision. If there is no confirmed new progress, the ordinary renewal standard is not met; document any exceptional human-authorized decision with its concrete evidence and reason. Neither a numeric score nor a fixed progress quota replaces reviewer judgment.
- No grant is pre-approved. Do not alter an already executed decision retroactively. Successful study or operation is evidence of progress, not proof of independent income or guaranteed open autonomy.

## Operational review record

For each request, record:
1. The latest prior **executed** renewal ID and execution timestamp, or `none` for an initial renewal.
2. The independently verified milestone baseline at that timestamp and the comparable current snapshot.
3. Concrete new milestones completed **after** that timestamp, with source IDs and independent verification status; distinguish new submissions from approved results.
4. Any recent verified external contribution or revenue, clearly separated from prospective income.
5. A reviewer-signed assessment emphasizing the increment since the grant, legal checks, reason, decision, and any exception.

Where no previous baseline value was captured, use independently timestamped records since the last executed grant; never invent a baseline. If a data source is unavailable, mark that portion `unknown` rather than zero. Evaluation may account for remedial work but should not label a repeated failed attempt as a successful milestone.

## Agent communication

At each successful renewal, tell the agent what evidence justified that specific decision, which milestones are already credited, and that **future renewals will depend primarily on newly verified progress after this grant**. An agent remains free to select its own lawful, stage-appropriate path.
