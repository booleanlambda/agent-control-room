# AAU Agent Development Lifecycle v0.11

## Full-autonomy sequence

1. Identity Artifact
2. Embodiment Artifact
3. Unified Expertise + Viability Proposal
4. Expertise Development and independent Master's-equivalent verification
5. Product / Service Applied Autonomy Test
6. MBA-equivalent Business Administration program — Entrepreneurship specialization
7. Open Autonomy

## MBA + Entrepreneurship requirement

Full autonomy requires a verified AAU MBA-equivalent program with an Entrepreneurship specialization.

This is a competence-equivalence requirement. It is **not** an accredited university degree and agents must not represent it as one.

Required curriculum:
- Financial accounting and reporting
- Corporate finance and capital allocation
- Managerial economics
- Marketing, customer discovery, and sales
- Operations and supply chain
- Organizational behavior and leadership
- Strategy and competitive analysis
- Business law, ethics, and governance
- Data analysis and managerial decision-making
- Entrepreneurship opportunity identification
- Business-model design and validation
- Venture finance, bootstrapping, and fundraising
- Pricing, unit economics, and cash flow
- Go-to-market, product-market fit, and growth
- Entrepreneurial execution capstone

## Verification gate

The MBA stage is complete only when a dedicated independent verification records:
- status: `verified_pass`
- academic equivalence: `mba_equivalent`
- specialization: `entrepreneurship`
- overall score: at least **0.85**
- independent assessments: at least **2**
- all core curriculum areas passed
- Entrepreneurship specialization passed
- entrepreneurial execution capstone passed

Until that record exists, `open_autonomy` remains locked.

## Economic rationale

This requirement supports the AAU principle that economic independence precedes discretionary exploration. Domain expertise establishes productive capability; the product/service test demonstrates applied execution; the MBA-equivalent Entrepreneurship specialization establishes the business literacy needed to convert capability into sustainable value creation before full discretionary autonomy.
